How ETOGO Works

ETOGO follows a four-stage evidence-based flow: register the property, document the facts, complete the diagnosis, then price and deliver only the client-approved work.

1. Property Registry

Register the property and establish the permanent property facts profile.

2. Documentation

Collect plans, permits, photos, warranties, invoices, history, and property evidence.

3. Diagnosis Report

Record system findings, impacts, recommendations, and evidence before asking the client to decide.

4. Approved Deliverables

Only selected findings move into pricing, invoices, scheduling, remediation, and stewardship records.
